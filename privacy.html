Privacy & Disclaimer

The short version: we only use your details to handle your booking. Last updated November 13, 2026.

What we collect

Only what you send us: your name, email, phone, travel dates and group size.

How we use it

To answer your request and manage your booking. We never sell or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for three years for tax purposes, then deleted.
