FAQ

The questions families ask us most, answered in plain language. Still stuck? Call us and a person picks up.

Booking

How do I book a home?

Send your dates, group size and the home you like from the contact page, or call our office. We reply within one business day with availability.

Are the rates per night?

Yes. The price shown on each home is per night. Rates and availability change with the season, so the quote we send when you book is the one we honor.

How many nights do I have to book?

Three nights minimum in peak season (June to August) and two nights the rest of the year. Longer stays get a discount — ask when you send your dates.

Can we add guests after booking?

Yes, up to the sleeps number listed for the home. Extra guests add a per-night fee, so tell us before arrival and we update your quote.

In the home

What is included in every rental?

Towels, beach chairs, an umbrella and a cooler are waiting in every home. You only need to pack clothes, sunscreen and food.

Do you provide baby gear?

Yes. Ask for a crib, high chair or baby gate when you book and we set it up before you arrive at no extra cost.

Can I bring my dog?

Two of our homes welcome one well-behaved dog. Tell us which pet you are bringing when you send your dates so we can match you to the right home.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Tell us your arrival time and we leave the key box open for late arrivals.

At the beach

How far is the beach?

Each home lists its own distance: some are on the sand, others are a one to three minute walk. Hidden cove homes have private beach access.

What if the weather turns bad?

If a storm makes the beach unusable for your whole stay, we move your dates once at no charge. Tell us before your second day.

Still have a question?

Send your dates and we reply within one business day, or call +639 489 312 5342 Mon–Sat, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
