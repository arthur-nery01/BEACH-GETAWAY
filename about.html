About Us

We are a local real estate agency that rents ocean view homes to families. This site helps you compare homes and book with us directly.

Our story

Beach Getaway started in 2012 with three cottages and one phone line. Today we manage ocean view homes along three stretches of coast.

We focus on families. Every home is close to calm water, has space for kids and is inspected by our team before each season.
