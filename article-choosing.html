How to Choose the Right Ocean View Rental

Beachfront, walk-to-beach or hidden cove: what each option means for your family.

1 min read

Beachfront

You step straight onto the sand. Best for families with young kids who need naps nearby.

Walk to beach

One to three minutes away. Usually quieter and lower priced, with the same view from the deck.

Hidden cove

Calm, clear water and few crowds. Ideal for snorkeling and older kids.
